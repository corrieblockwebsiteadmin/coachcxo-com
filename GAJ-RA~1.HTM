Co-Founder and Managing Partner of Kompass Consultancy, the AoEC's GCC and Levant delivery partner. A registered psychologist and high-performance coach who has worked with global corporates, government departments, educational institutions and sporting teams. Kompass states more than 15,000 coaching hours delivered internationally.

Why this placement

He is included on an explicitly different credential path from everyone else on this list, and the reasoning should be stated rather than glossed: he holds no ICF credential at all. What he holds instead is chartered psychology — MAPS with the Australian Psychological Society, MAICD with the Australian Institute of Company Directors — which is a longer and more regulated qualification route than any coaching certification.

He writes the Kompass blog and has kept it active through 2026, which is more sustained public teaching than most of this region's practitioners produce.

Editorial notes and exclusions

  • No ICF credential on any page checked — notable given his business partner holds PCC, and a real weakness against the credential criterion. He ranks tenth for that reason.
  • The registering jurisdiction for "Reg Psych" is not stated anywhere and could not be established.
  • "Recognised High Performance Coach" is site marketing copy, not an award. No books, no award verified.
