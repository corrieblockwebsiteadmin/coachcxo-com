Ranked #3 in the Q3 2026 edition of Top 10 Executive Coaches — GCC. Assessed against the published methodology.

Dr Clare Beckett-McInroy

Doha, Qatar

Founder, BECKETT MCINROY Consultancy; creator of the CoachME model and TAP360

Founder of BECKETT MCINROY Consultancy and originator of the CoachME coaching model and the TAP360 team profiling tool. She co-founded ICF Bahrain, where she was Vice President, and ICF Doha–Qatar, where she was President, and has contributed subject-matter expertise on team coaching and supervision to ICF headquarters. EdD, University of Bath.

Why this placement

MCC and ACTC corroborated on ICF's own person page, plus a doctorate from a serious university — an unusual combination in this field, where research training and coaching credentials rarely sit together. Her CoachME model and TAP360 instrument are published tools other practitioners use rather than proprietary language wrapped around standard practice.

CoachME was named ICF Distinguished Coaching Education Provider in 2023, confirmed verbatim on ICF's own past-winners page. She was also the nominating coach behind the Qatar Financial Centre's 2020 ICF Middle East Prism Award, documented in ICF's own published case study. Co-founding two national ICF chapters is infrastructure work that outlasts any individual practice.

Editorial notes and exclusions

  • The 2023 ICF award is organisational — it went to CoachME and the consultancy, not to her personally. Recorded accordingly.
  • "EMCC Coach of the Year" appears on her own site with no year attached and could not be verified. It is excluded.
  • ICF describes her as author of five books, but no publisher or year could be verified for any of them, and an earlier ICF profile said two. No book count is printed. A "Certified Agile Coach" credential names no issuing body. Her company is registered in Manchester, UK, while she practises from Doha.
