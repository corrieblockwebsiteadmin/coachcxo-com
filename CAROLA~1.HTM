Carolann Philips

Muscat, Oman

Executive coach and talent-development consultant; President, ICF Oman Chapter 2025–26

Executive coach, behavioural-skills trainer and talent-development consultant with more than 25 years across the Middle East and Asia, resident in Oman for over 30 years. Vice President of the ICF Oman Chapter from 2023 and President for the 2025–26 term. Her earlier career included learning and development at Bank Muscat.

Why this placement

Hers is the only credential in this entire study verified against the issuing body's own record. Her MCC appears on Credly, the platform ICF Credentials and Standards itself uses to issue badges — not on an ICF profile page, not in her own marketing, but at source. Everything else in this field rests on weaker evidence, and that distinction is worth more than a shelf of industry awards.

Her credential stack is unusually broad and independently issued — CPTD from the Association for Talent Development, GPHR from the HR Certification Institute, CELTA from Cambridge, SHL assessment certifications. Honestly, What Do I Do? was published by Notion Press in February 2026.

Editorial notes and exclusions

  • Four awards appear in her materials — from the World HRD Congress (2017) and the Middle East Training & Development Awards (2019). Both are commercial, pay-to-attend industry programmes with limited editorial standing, and they are recorded here as self-reported rather than as independent validation.
  • ICF Oman's board pages show her as PCC in 2023 and MCC in 2025–26, consistent with an upgrade; any credential reference should be dated.
  • She holds a dual role — personal practice plus Managing Director of Hallmark Training Institute. Disclosed.
