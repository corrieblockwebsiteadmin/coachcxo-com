Why this placement

Heading AoEC in the UAE is verifiable on AoEC's own site and is not a badge that can be bought — the distinction that separates real standing from purchased standing runs through this entire ranking, and she is on the right side of it. Kompass has been AoEC's delivery partner across the GCC and Levant since 2018.

Her client evidence is among the most concrete in the region: named testimonials from the CEO of Abu Dhabi Finance and the Chief of Corporate Affairs at the Dubai Future Foundation, plus a five-year partnership with INSEAD's Career Development for Working Professionals. Named senior clients willing to be quoted are rarer and more informative than award counts.
