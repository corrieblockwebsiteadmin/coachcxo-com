Sameera Ali Baba

Bahrain

Owner, Mubadara for Business Consultancy; Director of Learning and Lead CoachME Faculty MENA; past President, ICF Bahrain

Bahraini entrepreneur who built her career in training, delivering accredited coach education in both Arabic and English and designing programmes matched to clients' cultural context. Past President of ICF Bahrain, board member of the Bahrain Society for Training and Development, and Director of Learning and Lead CoachME Faculty for MENA at Beckett McInroy Consultancy.

Why this placement

MCC and ACTC corroborated on ICF's own person page, alongside EMCC Senior Practitioner and ESIA supervisor status. Delivering accredited coach training in Arabic is the substantive point: most coach education reaching the Gulf is imported and English-only, and building it in the working language of the region is a contribution the international recognition bodies have no way of seeing.

Her supervision accreditation matters more than it looks. Supervising other coaches is a generosity marker under this methodology — it is unpaid-in-reputation work that raises the standard of everyone else's practice.

Editorial notes and exclusions

  • Past presidency of ICF Bahrain is a peer-elected role, not an award. No award verified.
  • "SCRUM MASTER II" and "CMCC" appear in her credential string with no issuing body stated and are disregarded. A training award from HABC is self-stated with no year.
  • The "40+ workshops per year" figure is self-reported. No books.
